Design System — v1.0

CV System.

A print-first system for Scott Gemmell, Front-end UI Developer. Minimal & editorial, cool slate neutrals, sans set against mono.

Typeface Hanken Grotesk · Space Mono
Palette Slate ink · paper · one blue
Format A4 print · 20mm margins
01

Principles

01

Whitespace is the layout

Negative space does the structural work. Sections breathe; nothing is crowded to the edge. When in doubt, add space — not lines.

02

One quiet accent

Slate ink on cool paper carries the page. The blue appears only to guide the eye — a link, a single rule, a marker dot. Never decoration.

03

Mono signals system

Monospace marks the data — dates, labels, contact, page numbers — separating machine chrome from the human content set in sans.

04

Designed for grayscale

Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and space — not color. The CV must read perfectly on a black-and-white office printer.

RULE

Neutrals stay fixed; only the accent is swappable. Curated alternates at the same lightness & chroma: warm clay oklch(0.55 0.13 40), deep teal oklch(0.55 0.10 200). Everything must survive a grayscale printout.
