# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fintech recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads screening contract candidates. They scan dozens of CVs quickly — often on screen first, sometimes printed — and decide within seconds whether to read deeply. A secondary audience is agency recruiters keyword-matching for React/fintech contract roles.

## Product Purpose

A single-page CV site (scottgemmell.com) for Scott Gemmell, a front-end contract developer targeting well-paid fintech contracts. The page must (1) surface the banking/financial-services track record instantly, (2) demonstrate front-end craft by its own quality — the CV itself is the work sample, and (3) print/export cleanly to A4 PDF for agency submission. Success = recruiter contact for fintech contract roles.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, editorial, precise. Quiet confidence: whitespace-led structure, restrained single accent, monospace for data. The page should feel technically exact — like it was built by someone who sweats the details — without showing off.

## Anti-references

- **Generic template CV**: Canva/Word-template energy — skill rating bars, headshot-in-a-circle sidebars, icon soup, two-column cram.
- **Corporate SaaS landing page**: card grids, gradient accents, hero metrics, marketing-speak section scaffolding.

## Design Principles

1. **The CV is the work sample.** Every detail (semantics, a11y, print fidelity, performance) is evidence of competence; sloppiness anywhere undermines the pitch.
2. **Whitespace is the layout.** Negative space does the structural work; when in doubt add space, not lines (per DESIGN.md).
3. **One quiet accent.** Slate ink on cool paper carries the page; blue appears only to guide the eye.
4. **Mono signals system.** Monospace marks data — dates, labels, contact — separating chrome from human content.
5. **Survives grayscale and print.** Hierarchy from scale, weight, and space — never color alone; must read perfectly from a black-and-white office printer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Credibility requirement, not just compliance: the CV lists Accessibility as a core skill, so the page itself must pass scrutiny — contrast ≥4.5:1 for body text, visible focus states, skip link, reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks/headings, screen-reader-sane reading order.
